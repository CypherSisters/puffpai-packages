Recipe Box reads the JSON-LD shape that many recipe web pages carry. That shape follows the schema.org Recipe type (https://schema.org/Recipe). The schema.org vocabulary is published under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence. We read the format: the property names a page uses, like `recipeIngredient` and `recipeInstructions`. We copy no text from schema.org.

The unit sizes are the US customary measures (the US cup, the US fluid ounce, the avoirdupois ounce and pound), as published in public standards.

All words in this package, on its page and in its answers, were written by Cypher Sisters. No code was copied from anywhere.
