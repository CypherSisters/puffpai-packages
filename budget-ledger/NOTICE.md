OFX and QFX (Open Financial Exchange and its Quicken variant) and QIF (Quicken Interchange Format) are public bank download formats that banks offer to their customers. The OFX, QFX, QIF and CSV readers in this package are our own code, written for it; no reader or parser was copied from anywhere.

Ideas, not code, came from well-liked open budgeting tools: Actual Budget (MIT licence) and Firefly III (AGPL-3.0 licence), for things like rules that set a category by payee words, transfer pairs between your own accounts, and remembering a bank file's columns. No code was copied from either. Both are connector candidates: a later package could hand transactions to them for people who use them.

All words in this package, on its page and in its answers, were written by Cypher Sisters.
