Choose your region

Local Map keeps a street map of one area you choose, and a list of emergency places there (fire stations, hospitals, pharmacies, shelters, drinking water and more), in its own folder on this computer. Nothing leaves this computer until you press Check size (which reads only the map file's index) or Download. Then, one time, it contacts three hosts: build-metadata.protomaps.dev is asked only for the list of map builds; build.protomaps.com sends the street map, so it can tell which map area was fetched; overpass-api.de receives the corner coordinates of the area, rounded outward to a 0.05 degree grid, and sends the emergency places. All three see this computer's internet address. Nothing else about you is sent: not home, not your own points. Searching for a town below happens on this computer only. After the download, the map works with no internet.
