## Built from

- **HL7 FHIR R4.** The record shapes (Patient, Condition, MedicationStatement, AllergyIntolerance, Observation, Encounter, Immunization, and the Bundle used for export) follow the HL7 FHIR R4 resource definitions, https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/ . The FHIR specification is published under the Creative Commons "No Rights Reserved" (CC0) licence: https://hl7.org/fhir/license.html . HL7 and FHIR are registered trademarks of Health Level Seven International. This app is not endorsed by HL7.
- **International Patient Summary.** The one-page summary follows the sections of the International Patient Summary: problems, allergies and medicines (required), immunizations and results (recommended).
- **Ideas from Mere Medical** (MIT licence): keeping a household's medical records on your own device, and showing lab values over time.
- **Ideas from medTimer** (MIT licence): a plain medicine list with dose and how often.

Only ideas were taken from Mere Medical and medTimer; no code was copied from them. No code was copied from GPL projects.
