The plan's sections (the radios, the channels with what each is for, the people with their call signs, the times to check in, and a log of messages) follow the shape of the incident radio communications plan forms that emergency services use and publish as public forms.

All words in this package, on its page and in its answers, were written by Cypher Sisters. No text was copied from those forms, no frequency lists are included, and no code was copied from anywhere. The server's plumbing follows this project's own Emergency Plan package.
