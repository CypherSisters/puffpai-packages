# PuffPAI packages

The Cypher Sisters' own packages for the [PuffPAI Store](https://github.com/CypherSisters/puffpai-store). One folder per package, each with its own `puffpai.json` and README. The store's index points at a folder here and pins one commit.

| Folder | Kind | Version | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `alarmflow` | app | 2.1.0 | Timers and alarms. While BuildBot runs they ring here even with no page open: a notice card, and on Windows a beep through the speakers. |
| `calendar` | app | 2.1.0 | Events, tasks, medicines and bills for your household on one month view. Kept on this computer, never synced to an outside calendar. |
| `fitfam` | app | 2.1.0 | Workouts for each person in the household, with streaks, goals and a history that stays on this computer. |
| `freshtable` | app | 2.1.0 | Pantry, shopping list, meal plan and the recipes behind them. |
| `prep-supplies` | app | 1.0.0 | Track your emergency supplies (food, water, medicine, first aid, batteries, fuel and more) with quantities and expiry dates, see how many days the water and food would last your household, and what a basic 72-hour kit still lacks. It does not check weather alerts (the weather package does that), does not order anything, and does not remind you on its own; you ask, or look at the page. |
| `radio` | app | 1.0.0 | Internet radio: find stations by name, country, genre or language and play them on the World Radio page, with favorites. Sound plays only while that page is open in the Desktop; it does not play on a speaker by itself, and it has no podcasts or recording. |
| `trivia` | app | 2.1.0 | Quiz rounds with scores: play AeroTrivia on its page, or ask the bot to quiz you in chat. Kids mode, and room for questions of your own. |

It only works with an AdGuard Home you already run on this computer or your home network. It does not install or set up AdGuard Home, edit filter lists, change per-device settings, or remove rules it did not add. |
| `book-shelves` | tool | 1.0.0 | Find books in your Calibre library by title, author, series or tag, see one book's details, keep your own named shelves, and get a few plain suggestions of what to read next. | `document-search` | tool | 1.0.0 | Keeps the owner's documents (.txt, .md, .csv, .json, .docx, .html and other text files) on this computer and searches their words, so the bot can find and read them back. It matches words, not meanings (no embedding model), and it does not read PDFs, pictures or spreadsheets. |

| `jellyfin` | tool | 1.1.0 | Search your own Jellyfin library and play music, movies and shows on one of its players (a TV app, a phone, a browser tab): pause, skip, stop, volume, what is playing, and music playlists. | `network-devices` | tool | 1.0.0 | A list of the devices on your home network that this computer has talked to: address, hardware address, name if known, a maker guess, when each was first and last seen, and labels you give them. | `news-headlines` | tool | 1.1.0 | Reads the news feeds you add (RSS or Atom) and gives the latest headlines: title, source, time and link. It comes with no feeds, does not fetch or summarize articles, and each feed's site on the internet needs its own Yes before it loads. |
| `node-red-flows` | tool | 1.1.0 | Lists, runs, and turns on or off the flows in the Node-RED you already run, on this computer or your home network, and ships 14 ready-made home flows (news, weather, a daily briefing, Home Assistant routines and more) for you to import by hand. | `weather` | tool | 1.0.0 | The weather where you live: right now, a forecast for up to 7 days, and severe weather alerts in the United States. | `web-search` | tool | 1.1.0 | Searches the web through a SearXNG you run yourself, on this computer or your home network: web results and news, the top 8 each, with the title, a one-line snippet, the address and the engine that found it. Every app and tool ships off. Turn one on from Apps, PuffPAI store. A tool package has no page of its own: the brain uses it in chat.

**Check a package:** `puffpai check <folder>` from a PuffPAI install. Level 3 is the store's bar. Each package with a server also has its own `tests/test_server.py` (run with pytest from a PuffPAI install).

**Talking to something on your home network.** `home-assistant`, `jellyfin`, `adguard`, `node-red-flows` and `web-search` reach a service you run yourself. Each saves the address you give it (only an address on this computer or your home network) and keeps the key or login from the environment the Core sets, never on disk. A service on this same computer needs no further step. For one on another computer, the package asks the Core for a Yes card for that exact address and port (its manifest says `needs.addresses: lan`), nothing reaches it until you say Yes, and you can take the allowance back on the Bridge page or type an address there yourself. `news-headlines` works the same way for each feed's site on the internet (`needs.addresses: internet`). These six need a PuffPAI with that card (D110); an older PuffPAI does not take their manifests.

**Where these came from.** The five apps, the looks and the avatars were copied from BuildBotv2 `packages/` and are kept the same in both places. The tools, the two new apps and the skill were rewritten from the HomeBotFounders build (commit `b77fc40`) to the v2 package contract; each `server.py` names its source file on its first line. `look-clean` stays in this repo for history only: Clean became a built-in look (D90) and has no store row.
