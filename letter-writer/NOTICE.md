## Used for the rules (public domain or open government)

- **Federal plain language guidelines**, US government, https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language, public domain (US federal work). Used for: put the main point first, write to the reader as "you", plain words, short sentences and short paragraphs.
- **GOV.UK style guide**, UK Government Digital Service, https://www.gov.uk/guidance/style-guide, Open Government Licence v3.0. Used for: plain words, short sentences, front-load the important information, and avoid stock phrases.

The patterns for a complaint, a request and a cancellation (say what happened, what you have done, what you want and by when; give the reference number; ask for written confirmation) are common consumer-advice practice, written here in our own words.
